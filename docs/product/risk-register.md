# Technical & Product Risk Register

- **Tier:** 2 (supporting)
- **Last updated:** 2026-08-13

Living document. Reviewed at every RFC. Risks are not closed by being written
down. Severity gradings are engineering judgment, not external assessment.

| ID | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
|----|------|--------|-----------|------------|--------|
| R-01 | **Regulatory characterization is unknown.** How Ordina is classified in any given jurisdiction has not been assessed by counsel. Execution controls (C1) move Ordina toward order transmission, which is materially different from journaling or analysis in several regimes. Jurisdictions are undecided (Q-08). | Potentially existential | Unknown | Obtain counsel before any external beta or public release. No engineering document may state a legal conclusion. | **OPEN — owner: counsel** |
| R-02 | **Identity drift.** Each of the four "is not" items is a plausible user request and a plausible growth lever. The line gets crossed one reasonable request at a time, not deliberately. Since v0.3 the boundary rests on the **four pillars** (pre-commitment, deterministic derivation, provenance, agency), so drift now looks like erosion of determinism, not loss of user authorship. | Critical | **High** | Constitution §2.1 boundary test (five parts) in every RFC. Reviewers may reject on identity grounds alone. Controlled vocabulary in `glossary.md`. Navigation neutrality (`ux-constitution.md` §5E) closes the nearest new surface. | OPEN |
| R-09 | **Rules/AI boundary erosion.** The likeliest violation of C3 is gradual: AI-authored "Why?" explanations that begin to function as recommendations, or discretionary direction selection where Strategy logic is thin. Direction derivation (D2) is a new AI-adjacent surface; the guard is that Ordina must not infer direction where logic is absent. The boundary is a matter of *authority*, not wording, and hard to police by review alone. | Critical | **High** | E1 makes the boundary structural. `ux-constitution.md` §2 enumerates prohibited "Why?" behaviours; D2 forbids inferred direction. Internal quantities must be structurally unable to reach a status (E1, `glossary.md` §B). | OPEN |
| R-10 | **Status semantics drift.** WAIT quietly becoming a "soft no," or DO NOT TRADE becoming advisory, would hollow out the product with no visible feature change. | Critical | Medium | `validation-model.md` §4 is deterministic and normative. "Does this change status semantics?" is a mandatory RFC question. | OPEN |
| R-11 | **Market-data quality and latency.** Market context is a *blocking* validation input. Stale or wrong data produces confidently wrong statuses, and the WAIT/DO-NOT-TRADE split depends on distinguishing "temporarily unavailable" from "invalid" (Q-10a). **Escalated:** continuous re-validation (E7) and the pre-execution checkpoint (C10) evaluate data repeatedly, multiplying exposure to stale/invalid data driving a status change. | High | High | Q-10a must close before any market-data integration. Invalid data is a blocking failure by decision. | **OPEN — escalated** |
| R-05 | **Market-data cost and licensing.** Confirmed in scope (Q-03). Vendor terms commonly restrict storage and redistribution, which can conflict with C4 (evidence + provenance retention) and C7 export. **Escalated:** continuous re-validation (E7) multiplies data consumption and therefore cost and licensing surface. | High | Medium | No provider selection without an ADR that reads the actual licence terms against C4 and C7. | **OPEN — active/escalated** |
| R-08 | **Broker relationship and credential custody.** Execution controls require a live broker integration. Holding any credential makes Ordina a meaningful breach target, and gating (C1, C8, C10) must hold even under partial failure. **Updated:** D4 "broker-constraint adjustments" (Final Construction) is the first explicit broker touchpoint (P-03). | High | Medium | E4 requires structural gating at all four checkpoints. Broker selection requires an RFC and ADR covering credential storage, blast radius, and fail-closed behaviour. | OPEN |
| R-06 | **Discipline vs. engagement metrics.** Principle 9 and Constitution §5 mandate reduced screen time and accept thirty days of WAIT as success. Growth pressure will argue against both. | Medium | **High** | Constitutional, not a product decision. Removing friction requires amendment. `ux-constitution.md` §6 rules engagement metrics invalid as justification. | OPEN |
| R-04 | **Auditability vs. erasure.** C5 (auditable correction) and C7 (deletion workflow) plus statutory erasure rights are in tension. Reduced in severity by the decision to adopt logical audit integrity rather than physical immutability, but not eliminated. | Medium | Medium | Needs an ADR before the first persistent schema. Conditional on Q-08. | OPEN |
| R-07 | **Documentation rot.** A docs-first process that outlives its own discipline produces confidently wrong documents — worse than none. | Medium | Medium | Docs change in the same commit. ADRs superseded, never edited. Tier 5 documents hold no normative content, so they cannot drift into contradiction. | OPEN |
| R-12 | **Strategy authoring is the unowned centre.** Q-11b, Q-10b, and Q-16 converge on how a strategy is specified (Q-15 trader-readiness sourcing is RESOLVED — PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY and no longer part of this cluster). Building validation before strategy authoring is understood risks a rules engine that no User Zero can actually populate. | High | Medium | Treat strategy authoring as the first RFC candidate after ratification, ahead of the domain model. | OPEN |
| R-13 | **Risk/construction circularity** — Risk Validation needs constructed values, but construction was gated on READY. | High | — | Resolved by two-stage construction (Constitution C8; Preliminary precedes Risk Validation). | **RESOLVED — historical** |
| R-14 | **Modification as discipline bypass.** With outputs non-overridable (C9), the residual vector is **input-fishing**: repeatedly re-tuning inputs to manufacture a desired output — the SNR-entry violation WN-0003 warns of, achieved indirectly. | Medium | Medium | C9 forces full re-validation on every input change; each revised decision is auditable (C5). Learning may surface repeated re-tuning for review. | OPEN |
| R-16 | **Preliminary/Final divergence.** If permitted Final-Construction adjustments (freshness, rounding, tick-size, broker) are not tightly bounded, Final can drift from the risk-validated Preliminary, and a trade could execute on parameters risk never approved. | High | Medium | E8 requires deterministic, revalidated adjustments only. P-02/P-03 must bound them. C10 checkpoint re-checks risk before execution. | OPEN |
| R-17 | **Checkpoint churn.** A pre-execution checkpoint (C10) that repeatedly flips READY→WAIT→READY could produce notification thrash and decision fatigue, undermining Principle 9 (reduce screen time). | Medium | Medium | Notification frequency/suppression is a deferred product question (`trading-decision-model.md` §10). Neutral, non-urgent language required (`ux-constitution.md` §5D). | OPEN |
| R-15 | **Account/exposure data dependency.** Risk Validation now formally consumes account and exposure state (N-07), a data source not previously in scope. Intersects broker credential custody (R-08). | High | Medium | Source undecided (N-07); no selection without an RFC/ADR covering provenance, freshness, and custody. | OPEN |
| R-20 | **Authoring assistance becomes Strategy origination** (was RFC-0001 R-A; extends R-02, R-09). Assistive authoring (wizard/template/visual/NL) can drift from helping the Trader express a Strategy into Ordina originating discretionary operative content. | Critical | Medium | Trader authors and owns the Strategy; operative rules explicit and inspectable; Trader confirmation required; AI has no rule/status authority; no silent discretionary content. `rfc/0001-strategy-formation-and-authoring.md` §9, §16. No mechanism selected. | OPEN |
| R-21 | **Well-Formedness auto-repair temptation** (was RFC-0001 R-B). A checker designed to automatically fix incomplete or conflicting rules would make Ordina originate operative semantics. | High | Medium | Detect, report, explain, request Trader correction; **never silently repair** operative rules (RFC-0001 §12). | OPEN |
| R-22 | **Condition-classification ambiguity reaches runtime** (was RFC-0001 R-C; adjacent to R-10). Absent, ambiguous, conflicting, or runtime-mutable BLOCKING/TEMPORARY classification undermines deterministic status semantics. | High | Medium | Exactly one static classification per condition per Strategy revision; reject invalid classification at Well-Formedness; runtime evaluates satisfaction only (RFC-0001 §10, §12). | OPEN |
| R-23 | **Revision / historical reproducibility gap** (was RFC-0001 R-D; C5). If decisions are not bound to exact Strategy revisions, later changes could alter the interpretation or reproducibility of historical decisions. | High | Medium | Distinct revision identity; decision provenance binds the exact revision; no retroactive mutation of historical decisions (RFC-0001 §13). No persistence architecture selected. | OPEN |
| R-24 | **User-Zero authoring oversimplifies ordered evidence** (was RFC-0001 R-E; D11). An authoring model optimized for simplicity may fail to express required context/location/sequence/timing/confirmation semantics. | Medium | Medium | Authoring interaction may be simple, but authoritative operative semantics must remain complete and inspectable; do not remove required Strategy expressiveness to simplify UI (RFC-0001 §17). | OPEN |
| R-18 | **Self-assessment gaming.** Self Assessment is the primary blocking readiness input (PBD-0003 D1), so a trader under FOMO, revenge, or emotional pressure can falsely self-report "ready" and pass Trader Readiness. This is a known residual of preserving trader agency. | Medium | High | **Must not** be solved by letting objective or probabilistic signals override Self Assessment (D2). Objective evidence may warn and prompt confirmation only (N-15). Learning may surface patterns for review (Constitution §6). | OPEN |
| R-19 | **Readiness-data privacy and dependency.** Optional Objective Readiness Evidence (wearables, sleep, calendar, behavioural history) would introduce privacy, consent, jurisdiction, and external-data dependencies (Q-08, C7). | High | — | **Conditional / deferred** until such integrations are authorized. No provider or integration selected. Requires an RFC/ADR and counsel input before any collection (N-16). | **DEFERRED — conditional** |
| R-25 | **Canonicalization incompleteness** (ADR-0003 §15). A non-total/non-deterministic canonical form lets semantically-equivalent artifacts diverge in revision identity. *Highest-severity architecture risk.* | High | — | Totality/determinism required as an acceptance criterion; compositional/recursive canonicalization (ADR-0003 §6). Mechanism deferred. | OPEN |
| R-26 | **Canonicalization-version drift** (ADR-0003 §15). Changing canonicalization rules silently re-identifies existing revisions. | High | — | Canonicalization version is identity-bearing (ADR-0003 I6). | OPEN |
| R-27 | **Erasure / reproducibility loss** (ADR-0003 §15). Authorized physical erasure of canonical content destroys historical reproduction. | Medium | — | Explicit post-erasure state; identity-as-tombstone; reproduction loss must be explicit (ADR-0003 §8, I10). Retention/privacy mechanism deferred (Q-08). | OPEN |
| R-28 | **Config / revision mis-placement** (ADR-0003 §15). In-bounds configuration folded into revision identity, or an operative change excluded from it. | High | — | Exhaustive ID/CX/PX/INV placement + closure rule; configuration structure-preservation invariant (ADR-0003 §6, §6.1, §6.2). | OPEN |
| R-29 | **Rule-ID instability** (ADR-0003 §15). Unstable rule/node identity breaks provenance and explanation. | Medium | — | Dual-identity (lineage + revision) with revision-qualified references (ADR-0003 §9). | OPEN |
| R-30 | **Well-Formedness / runtime semantic divergence** (ADR-0004 §15, W-R1). A Strategy passes Well-Formedness but the runtime evaluator interprets it differently. | High | — | The §9 congruence invariant — checker-semantics version and the future evaluator-semantics version must be conforming realizations of the same **Canonical Semantic Contract**; shared canonical semantic model and admitted construct set (ADR-0004 §9). Evaluator not selected; N-24 remains OPEN. | OPEN |
| R-31 | **Intractable-but-decidable analysis** (ADR-0004 §15, W-R2). Pathological but decidable Strategies exhaust resources during Well-Formedness analysis. | High | — | Bounded-analysis admission: decidability + termination + tractability-boundedness; constructs whose required analyses are unbounded are **inadmissible**, not deferred (ADR-0004 §4). Architectural bound, not an implementation resource budget. | OPEN |
| R-32 | **Cross-implementation checker divergence** (ADR-0004 §15, W-R3). Two conforming Well-Formedness checkers disagree on the verdict, defect set, or defect ordering. | High | — | Deterministic Well-Formedness specification, contract-bound verdict-affecting normalization, deterministic defect identity and canonical defect ordering (ADR-0004 §7, §10, §12). | OPEN |
| R-33 | **Checker-semantics-version drift** (ADR-0004 §15, W-R4). A change to checker semantics silently changes Well-Formedness verdicts. | High | — | The checker-semantics version is bound to every WF result and encompasses the applicable Canonical Semantic Contract version; any change to authoritative WF semantics requires a version change (ADR-0004 §8, §10). | OPEN |
| R-34 | **Well-Formedness bypass** (ADR-0004 §15, W-R5). A Strategy reaches canonicalization, revision identity, or runtime without passing the Well-Formedness gate. | High | — | Non-bypassable gate — no path to canonicalization/identity/runtime skips WF (ADR-0004 §12; ADR-0003 I9). Enforcement mechanism deferred downstream (ADR-0004 §18 o-1). | OPEN |
